BigWave Radio for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later)

1. Drag "BigWave Radio" onto the Applications folder.
2. The app is not notarised by Apple yet, so the first time:
   right-click (or Control-click) BigWave Radio in Applications > Open > Open.
   If macOS only offers "Move to Bin" / "Done": open System Settings >
   Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway".
   Or in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/BigWave Radio.app"

Your library, ratings, settings and plugins live in
~/Library/Application Support/BigWaveRadio
There is no menu-bar icon yet: closing the window quits.
